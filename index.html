The first step in taking a finished playable to Google Ads. Each file is scored against the App-campaign spec and the Google Ads policies, and Fix all converts what can be converted without a person: the exit call, the SDK stand-in, the exitapi.js tag, storage, dialogs, sound before touch, metas and filenames. What it cannot decide is listed per file for you to judge. The result is a file with a far better chance of running correctly and staying clear of policy trouble. That is an improvement in the odds, not a promise of approval.

How the AI policy review works

What it does

An AI looks at your creative the way a reviewer would, and cross-checks it against the Google Ads policies: prohibited content, prohibited practices, restricted content, and editorial and technical standards.

This matters most for playables that draw their words instead of writing them. A canvas creative renders every headline and button label into an image, so the automatic copy checks have nothing to read. The AI is the only pass here that can see those words.

What gets sent

  • Every visible text string found in the file.
  • One downscaled contact sheet of the creative's largest images, numbered so a finding can point at one. You can see the exact sheet under “What the model saw” after a review.
  • Google's policy text, so the model judges against the policies rather than its own recollection.

The file itself is never sent, and neither is full-resolution artwork.

How findings are judged

The model is told to judge only against the supplied policy text, and to quote the sentence it is relying on. Each quote is checked against the source before it is shown, so a paraphrased or invented one loses its claim to be Google's wording and is marked unsourced instead. Treat unsourced findings as a prompt to check, not as a rule.

Your key

  • Nothing is sent until you paste a key and press a review button.
  • The key goes straight from this page to the service you chose, and nowhere else.
  • It is remembered only in this browser, separately per service.
  • Usually the key alone is enough. A Google, Anthropic or Zhipu key identifies its own service, and each service already has a model selected. Only a bare sk-… is ambiguous, because OpenAI, Kimi and Qwen share that shape, so pick the service yourself in that case.
  • Every model offered reads images, because judging the artwork is the point.

What it is not

Findings are advisory and never change the readiness score, which stays deterministic and reproducible. The AI can miss things and can be wrong. It raises the odds of catching a policy problem before Google does; it does not promise an approval.
